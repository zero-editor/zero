Four directions

Same state, same tab, different signal
01 / Fraction + spinnerRecommended

The number says exactly how far things have come; the ring keeps saying work is still happening. Both facts fit in the existing status slot.

Exact countCompactWorks with many processes
02 / Segmented ring
zero×

One segment per process. Finished segments light up while a small orbiting point marks the remaining activity. Quiet, glanceable, and number-free.

Visual progressBest for 2–6 processes
03 / Separate counts
zero×

A check count and a spinner count sit side by side. The most literal option: “2 finished, 2 working” needs no denominator or explanation.

Both countsClearestWidest
04 / Process pips
zero×

One tiny bar per process, filled when finished and gently outlined while working. More legible than dots at this scale, but less scalable than a count.
